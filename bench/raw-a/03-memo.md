MEMO — Collections Risk Committee
Date: 2026-09-28
To: Collections operations, digital channels
Subject: Promise windows, Q4 2026

The committee reviewed promise-to-pay kept rates for Q3. Promises made by customers 8 to 45 days late, more than a week out, were kept far less often than shorter ones.

Decision: for customers 8 to 45 days late, the promise deadline is the 5th business day after the day of the call. The number of persuasion attempts and every other rule stay as they are.

For customers 1 to 7 days late nothing changes.

This applies to all outbound calls placed from 1 October 2026.
